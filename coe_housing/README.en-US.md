# coe_housing

Residential and commercial properties: starter apartment, hotels, houses, and business locations. Registered in the admin **Properties** tab (`coe_housing_registry`).

- **Property:** type (`apartment`, `hotel`, `house`, `commercial`), entrance, interior point, number of units, and blip. Each unit is an independent lease.
- **Interior in an individual world:** all residents use the same interior (MLO/IPL), with each lease in its own routing bucket (`BucketBase + lease id`).
- **Starter property:** marked as `starter`, free, and with no rent. The character receives one unit only once, on load (`coe_core:characterLoaded` event, `coe_housing_grants` table). If no starter property is registered, the character receives one at the next login after it is created.
- **Rent:** fixed daily rate per property and weekly, biweekly, and monthly cycles. The cycle is charged in advance through `coe_economy` and automatically renewed when due; if there are insufficient funds, there are `GraceDays` (3) of tolerance, during which entry is blocked, after which the lease is terminated. The contracted daily rate is locked in the lease. Cancellation is not refunded.
- **Limits:** `MaxTenancies` (3) leases per character, one per property.
- **Commercial property (`commercial`):** the tenant is a business (`coe_businesses`), in `coe_housing_leases`, with its own world (`CommercialBucketBase + lease id`). The owner and team of the tenant business can enter, even when off-site. Two ways to occupy:
  - **Assigned by admin:** the "Linked business" (`businessId`) field creates a free lease for one unit, with no rent and no cancellation; changing or removing the link ends the assigned lease.
  - **Rented:** someone clocked in at the business with `business.funds` can rent at the entrance for the business where they are clocked in. The cycle is charged to the society account (`coe_economy:SocietyCharge`) and automatically renewed; if there are insufficient funds, the clocked-in team is notified and the lease ends after the grace period. Cancellation requires `business.funds` and is not refunded. Limit of `MaxLeasesPerBusiness` (3) rented properties per business, one per property.
  - The business tablet lists the business's properties under Properties: the `properties` provider is registered with `coe_businesses:RegisterProvider` (`BusinessProperties`, only for `coe_businesses`).

- **Illegal property (`hideout`):** a hidden entrance leading to another space (e.g. a basement under a house). It has no blip, rent, or units and is not listed by the real estate agency; the entrance is sent only to the `owner` and people with access (`members`, up to `Limits.MaxHideoutMembers`), and the prompt enters directly. Everyone shares the same world (`HideoutBucketBase + world`, a fixed number assigned on the first save) and a shared stash (`stash:housing:<property>:shared`). Anyone who loses access (removed, property deactivated or deleted) is moved out immediately; someone who left the game inside returns inside only if they still have access. The owner manages people in the tablet (`/casa` → **Hideout**, exports `TabletVisible` and `TabletCall` with `list`, `allow {property, stateId}`, and `revoke {property, characterId}`, for `coe_tablet` only).
- **Rentals app (COE OS):** anyone with a personal lease or on the team of a business with a commercial lease sees the **Rentals** app on the tablet (`TabletVisible(playerId, 'rentals')`). The `list` action returns each lease with property, type, unit, payer (you or the business), daily rate, cycle, next charge or termination date, state, and entrance location for GPS.
- **Property stash:** a point marked by the admin inside the interior (up to `Stash.MaxDistanceFromInterior` from the interior point), with slots and weight per property (defaults by type in `Stash.Defaults`). Each resident has their own stash (`stash:housing:<property>:c<character>`), including in the starter apartment; commercial properties have one per business (`stash:housing:<property>:b<business>`). Contents are tied to the property, not the lease: anyone who rents again finds what they stored. Opens via `coe_inventory:OpenStash`, only for someone inside with a valid lease. Capacity applies to new stashes.
- **Native interior catalog** (`shared/interiors.lua`, `HousingInteriors`): apartments, houses, offices, warehouses, garages, and base-map locations, by category. Each property can specify an `interiorId`; when entering, the client loads that interior's IPLs and entity sets (removing variants in the same group) and waits for the interior to be ready. The saved interior point remains the destination; catalog coordinates are a starting point and should be checked in-game ("Visit and adjust" in admin).
  - Thumbnails are generated in-game by the admin and stored in `coe_housing_interior_thumbs` (migration v4; base64 JPEG, up to `Limits.MaxThumbBytes`). Requires the `screenshot-basic` resource to be started.
  - Server exports (for `coe_admin` only): `Interiors`, `InteriorThumbs`, `SaveInteriorThumb`, `BeginPreview`, and `EndPreview` (visit in an isolated world, `PreviewBucketBase + player id`, without population; denied when inside a property).
- **Real estate agency (admin):** exports `PropertyContracts(propertyId)` (property leases: unit, resident or business, daily rate, cycle, status, and number of people inside) and `EndContract(kind, id)` (ends a resident or business lease and notifies anyone who was on the lease; refuses the starter apartment and a property assigned to a business). For `coe_admin` only.
- **Deliveries and spaces (`DeliveryCallers`, currently `coe_amzg` and `coe_deepweb`):** address = property + space owner (`c<character>`, `b<business>`, or `shared`, same as the stash). Exports: `DeliveryAddresses(playerId)` (residential properties, commercial properties for businesses where the character works, and illegal properties the character can access, excluding expired leases), `AddressActive(propertyId, owner)`, `CurrentSpace(playerId)` (`{ propertyId, owner, interior }` of the person inside), and `PlayersInSpace(propertyId, owner)`.

Usage: `E` at the entrance opens the menu (enter, rent, cancel); `E` at the interior point exits; `E` at the stash opens it.

Not yet implemented: listings, rent and resale between players, visits, and residential garages.

Known limitation: none for logging out inside the interior. Anyone who disconnects (or is online when the server restarts) inside returns to the interior in the same individual world (`coe_housing_presence`); if the lease ended in the meantime, they reappear at the entrance. Because character spawning finishes later and would place them at their last street position, the client reapplies the teleport to the interior immediately after `playerSpawned` (also applies when respawning after death inside the property). If the player still ends up at the entrance marked as inside, using the entrance makes the server clear the presence (`housing_presence_recovered`) and they can enter normally.

Admin visit limitation: if the admin disconnects during a visit or thumbnail generation, they return at the interior point in the normal world.
